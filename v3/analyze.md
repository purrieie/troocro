# See what TrooCRO would fix on your store first.

TrooCRO reads your page, checks it against the TrooCRO signal library, tags every failure to a buyer barrier and plans your first tests. Takes about 2 minutes. No login, nothing to install.

## What we check
- Conversion review (40% of the score): the TrooCRO signals that apply to the page, each failure tagged to one of 79 buyer barriers.
- Speed (25%): Google PageSpeed on mobile when available, otherwise estimated from the page's code.
- Search and AI answers (15%): whether search engines and AI assistants can read, understand and recommend the store.
- Accessibility (20%): labels, alt text, language and contrast.
- The weakest area pulls the total down further. Basics every store needs earn nothing when present and cost a lot when missing.
- How AI agents see the page (shown, not scored).
- Growth stack (shown, not scored): analytics, heatmaps, ad pixels, retention, reviews and testing tools.

## Actions
Forms on this page, and what they send:

### Analyze a site
- Method: GET
- `url` (text): Your website. Opening analyze.html?url=yourstore.com starts the scan.

### Quick questions (optional)
- `category`: What you sell (Beauty & Personal Care, Fashion & Apparel, Jewellery & Accessories, Health & Wellness, Food & Beverages, Home & Living, Electronics & Gadgets, Baby & Kids, Pet Care, Sports & Fitness, Games & Toys)
- `aov`: Typical order value (Under Rs 500; Rs 500 to 2,000; Rs 2,000 to 5,000; Over Rs 5,000)
- `goal`: More orders, bigger baskets, better return on ad spend, a faster store, or showing up in AI answers
- `visitors`: Monthly visitors to the page
- `competitors`: Up to two competitor sites

Shared reports open at analyze.html?r=REPORT_ID.
